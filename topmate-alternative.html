Brandzy vs Topmate: a Topmate alternative with 0% commission

Updated 15 November 2026

Short answer

Topmate is free to start but keeps 10% of every sale from your profile (20% when a buyer comes through its marketplace). Brandzy is a flat ₹999 a month with 0% commission, so once you sell more than about ₹10,000 a month, Brandzy costs less. Brandzy also adds Instagram Auto DM, a live media kit, brand deals and invoices.

Which should you choose?

Topmate: Choose Topmate if you mostly sell calls, sell only occasionally, or want buyers from its marketplace. With no monthly fee, it's cheaper while your sales are small.

Brandzy: Choose Brandzy if you sell regularly (above about ₹10,000 a month the flat price wins), work with brands, or want Instagram Auto DM to send your link from comments.

Frequently asked questions

How much commission does Topmate take?

Topmate's pricing page lists 10% on every sale made through your profile or link and 20% on sales through its marketplace, with no monthly fee (checked 15 November 2026).

Is there a Topmate alternative with no commission?

Yes. Brandzy charges a flat ₹999 a month (Growth) and takes 0% of your sales; buyers pay you directly by UPI.

When is Brandzy cheaper than Topmate?

When you sell more than about ₹10,000 a month: 10% of ₹10,000 is ₹1,000, roughly Brandzy Growth's price.
