Instagram DM automation tools for Indian creators (free and paid)

Updated 15 November 2026. Competitor prices checked 15 November 2026; they change, so check each site before deciding.

Short answer

For free, use Instagram's own saved replies and conversation starters (FAQs), which you trigger yourself. To send your link automatically when someone comments a keyword, you need a tool on the official Instagram API: ManyChat (US-dollar plans that grow with contacts) or Brandzy (₹999 a month, with a store, media kit and brand deals included).

1. Instagram's built-in tools (free)

Saved replies let you paste a prepared answer in two taps, and FAQs (conversation starters) show suggested questions when someone opens a chat with you. Nothing is sent automatically when someone comments.

2. ManyChat

A dedicated chat automation tool with comment and keyword triggers and multi-step flows across several channels. It has a free plan with a small contact limit; paid plans are in US dollars and grow with the number of contacts. Brandzy vs ManyChat.

3. Brandzy Auto DM

Sends your link by DM when someone comments a keyword on your post or Reel, or messages it to you. Official Instagram API, approved by Meta. 1 rule on Growth (₹999/month), unlimited on Pro (₹1,999/month), with a store, media kit and brand deals in the same plan. How it works.

4. Avoid: password-based bots

Any tool that asks for your Instagram password or automates your account from outside the official API breaks Instagram's rules and can get your account restricted.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free Instagram comment-to-DM tool?

Instagram's own features (saved replies, FAQs) are free but not automatic. Automatic comment-to-DM tools on the official API have small free tiers or paid plans; Brandzy has a 7-day free trial.

Is Instagram DM automation allowed?

Yes, through the official Instagram API with your permission, which is how Brandzy and ManyChat work. Password-based bots aren't allowed.
