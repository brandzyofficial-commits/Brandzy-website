Best ways to sell digital products in India as a creator (fees compared)

Updated 15 November 2026. Competitor prices checked 15 November 2026; they change, so check each site before deciding.

Short answer

If you sell occasionally, a per-sale platform like Topmate (10%) or Cosmofeed (10% + GST) costs nothing until you sell. If you sell every month, a flat price wins: Brandzy is ₹999 a month with 0% commission and UPI paid straight to you. Stan Store is $29 a month in US dollars.

1. Brandzy

₹999/month (Growth), 0% commission. 17 product types, course builder, memberships, 1:1 calls with Google Calendar, UPI paid straight to you (you confirm each payment), plus Instagram Auto DM, media kit and brand deals. Store features.

2. Topmate

No monthly fee; 10% of each sale through your profile, 20% through its marketplace. Strong for calls and marketplace discovery. Brandzy vs Topmate.

3. Cosmofeed

10% platform fee plus GST per sale (help centre), lower on paid yearly plans; payments confirmed automatically. Brandzy vs Cosmofeed.

4. Instamojo

A payment gateway with a store: free plan takes 10% + ₹3 on digital products plus GST on fees. Good if you need a full gateway. Brandzy vs Instamojo.

5. Stan Store

$29 or $99 a month in US dollars, 0% platform fee, card checkout. Brandzy vs Stan Store.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest way to sell digital products in India?

Below about ₹8,000–10,000 a month in sales, a per-sale platform (Topmate, Cosmofeed) costs less. Above that, a flat price with 0% commission, like Brandzy at ₹999 a month, costs less.

Can I sell digital products with UPI?

Yes. Brandzy's checkout lets buyers pay to your UPI ID directly; you confirm the payment and access unlocks.
