Pricing & commission disclosure

1. Early access: free

Brandzy is currently free to use during early access. There is no charge to track deals, generate invoices, manage clients, or build your media kit.

2. We never take a cut of your brand deals

Brandzy is a tool, not a payment processor or marketplace. Money from a brand deal goes directly from the brand to you — it never passes through Brandzy. That means Brandzy does not, and will not, take a percentage or commission on any payment you receive from a brand, regardless of pricing plan.

3. Future paid plans

We plan to introduce an optional paid plan (e.g. a subscription) that unlocks additional features. Details are not final. When a paid plan launches:

  • Pricing will be clearly displayed in-app and on this page before you're asked to pay;
  • Any free features you already rely on will not be paywalled without clear notice;
  • No fee will ever be calculated as a percentage of your brand-deal earnings — pricing will be a flat subscription, not a commission.

4. Questions

If you have questions about pricing, reach out at info@brandzy.app.
