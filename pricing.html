Pricing

Last updated: 11 October 2026

1. Plans

Every new account starts with a 7-day free trial with full access to every Pro feature. Cancel before the trial ends and you won't be charged. After the trial, an active plan is needed to keep using your dashboard. One free trial per person.

Growth — ₹999/month in India, US$12/month outside India

  • Brand deal pipeline and client CRM
  • Invoicing and payment tracking
  • Public media kit link with brand inquiry form
  • Brand inquiry alerts
  • Public storefront page with unlimited product listings (digital downloads, courses, coaching, memberships)
  • Built-in checkout: UPI paid straight to your UPI ID, plus card payments from buyers outside India through your own Razorpay, Stripe or PayPal payment link
  • Email list of your buyers and subscribers
  • Instagram Auto DM with 1 active keyword rule

Pro — ₹1,999/month in India, US$24/month outside India

  • Everything in Growth
  • Email broadcasts and email automations (welcome series, after-purchase follow-ups)
  • Verified YouTube and Instagram stats, synced daily, with a "Verified" badge on your media kit
  • Unlimited Instagram Auto DM keyword rules
  • Priority support
  • Advanced revenue and deal analytics (coming soon)
  • Team seats for managers and assistants (coming soon)
  • Custom domain support (coming soon)

Features marked "coming soon" are not yet available. Pro subscribers get them at no extra cost when they launch.

2. Billing

  • Plans come in packs of 1, 3, 6 or 12 months. 3 months saves about 5%, 6 months about 10%, and 12 months costs the same as 10 (two months free).
  • Pay once or auto-renew, your choice. Paying once covers the pack and nothing renews; we email you before your plan ends. With auto-renew, the same pack is charged again at the end of each period by UPI Autopay or card, until you turn it off in Settings. You are notified before each auto-renew charge.
  • Creators in India pay in INR: one-time payments through Cashfree Payments (UPI, cards, and net banking), and auto-renew through Razorpay (UPI Autopay or cards). UPI Autopay covers charges up to ₹15,000, so the 12-month Pro pack needs a card for auto-renew. Prices shown in INR are inclusive of applicable taxes unless stated otherwise at checkout.
  • New accounts get a 7-day free trial. We may ask you to set up auto-pay to start it; nothing is charged until the trial ends, and turning auto-renew off during the trial means you are never charged.
  • Creators outside India pay in US dollars by international debit or credit card through Razorpay, once or on auto-renew, with the same features and the same 1, 3, 6 and 12-month packs: Growth US$12 / US$34 / US$65 / US$120 and Pro US$24 / US$68 / US$130 / US$240. Your bank may add a foreign-exchange fee. Prices in US dollars do not include any taxes your country may charge.
  • Turn auto-renew off at any time in Settings: no further charges are made and you keep the time already paid for. You can request a refund within 14 days of any charge. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

3. What Brandzy sells

Brandzy sells one thing: a software subscription, the Growth and Pro plans above. Brandzy is a tool, not a payment processor, marketplace or donation platform. We do not accept tips or donations, and we never collect, hold or pass on money for creators.

Money from a brand deal or a storefront sale goes directly from the brand or customer to the creator. It never passes through Brandzy, and we charge no percentage or commission on what creators earn.
