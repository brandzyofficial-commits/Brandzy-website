Pricing

Last updated: 3 October 2026

1. Plans

Every new account starts with a 7-day free trial with full access to every Pro feature. Cancel before the trial ends and you won't be charged. After the trial, an active plan is needed to keep using your dashboard. One free trial per person.

Growth — ₹999/month (US$12/month outside India)

  • Brand deal pipeline and client CRM
  • Invoicing and payment tracking
  • Public media kit link with brand inquiry form
  • Brand inquiry alerts
  • Public storefront page with unlimited product listings (digital downloads, courses, coaching, memberships)
  • Built-in UPI checkout, paid straight to your UPI ID
  • Email list of your buyers and subscribers
  • Instagram Auto DM with 1 active keyword rule (coming soon)

Pro — ₹1,999/month (US$24/month outside India)

  • Everything in Growth
  • Email broadcasts and email automations (welcome series, after-purchase follow-ups)
  • Verified YouTube and Instagram stats, synced daily, with a "Verified" badge on your media kit
  • Unlimited Instagram Auto DM keyword rules (coming soon)
  • Priority support
  • Advanced revenue and deal analytics (coming soon)
  • Team seats for managers and assistants (coming soon)
  • Custom domain support (coming soon)

Features marked "coming soon" are not yet available. Pro subscribers get them at no extra cost when they launch.

2. Billing

  • Plans are prepaid: pay for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months at a time. 3 months saves about 5%, 6 months about 10%, and 12 months costs the same as 10 (two months free). Nothing renews automatically; we email you before your plan ends.
  • Creators in India pay in INR through Cashfree Payments (UPI, cards, and net banking). Prices shown in INR are inclusive of applicable taxes unless stated otherwise at checkout.
  • Creators outside India pay the USD price (US$12 for Growth, US$24 for Pro), billed through our international payment partner, which acts as the merchant of record and adds any applicable sales tax or VAT at checkout.
  • There is no auto-debit to cancel, and you can request a refund within 14 days of a charge. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

3. We never take a cut of your earnings

Brandzy is a tool, not a payment processor or marketplace. Money from a brand deal or a storefront sale goes directly from the brand or customer to you — it never passes through Brandzy. Your subscription is a flat monthly fee; we do not charge any percentage or commission on what you earn.
