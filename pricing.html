Pricing

Last updated: 27 September 2026

1. Plans

Growth — ₹999/month

  • Brand deal pipeline and client CRM
  • Invoicing and payment tracking
  • Public media kit link with brand inquiry form
  • Brand inquiry alerts
  • Public storefront page with unlimited product listings (digital downloads, coaching, memberships)

Pro — ₹1,999/month

  • Everything in Growth
  • Priority support
  • Advanced revenue and deal analytics (coming soon)
  • Team seats for managers and assistants (coming soon)
  • Custom domain support (coming soon)

Features marked "coming soon" are not yet available. Pro subscribers get them at no extra cost when they launch.

2. Billing

  • Plans are billed monthly and renew automatically until you cancel.
  • Creators in India pay in INR through Razorpay (UPI, cards, and net banking). Prices shown in INR are inclusive of applicable taxes unless stated otherwise at checkout.
  • Creators outside India are billed in USD through our international payment partner, which acts as the merchant of record and adds any applicable sales tax or VAT at checkout.
  • You can cancel at any time, and you can request a refund within 14 days of a charge. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

3. We never take a cut of your earnings

Brandzy is a tool, not a payment processor or marketplace. Money from a brand deal or a storefront sale goes directly from the brand or customer to you — it never passes through Brandzy. Your subscription is a flat monthly fee; we do not charge any percentage or commission on what you earn.

4. Questions

If you have questions about pricing, reach out at info@brandzy.app.
