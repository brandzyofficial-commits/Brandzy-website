1. Brandzy: best for creators who work with brands

Brandzy is built in India for YouTubers and Instagram creators whose income comes from sponsorships as well as products. One link shows your media kit (audience numbers, rate card, brands you have worked with) and your store. Behind it you get:

  • A deal pipeline from inquiry to paid, with stages that move on their own as deliverables are approved and invoices are paid.
  • Brand inquiries from your page turned into deals automatically, with a notification.
  • Invoices you can send by email or WhatsApp, plus payment reminders.
  • A client CRM, deliverable deadlines, and sending content to the brand for approval.
  • A store with 17 categories, from e-books and presets to 1:1 calls, merch and a tip jar.

Price: ₹999 (Growth) or ₹1,999 (Pro) a month, paid by UPI, card or net banking. No commission. Missing today: built-in card checkout for store orders (coming soon) and a course builder.

2. Stan Store: best for selling courses

Stan Store is a popular link-in-bio store with a course builder, bookings, email marketing and Instagram AutoDM. It takes no platform commission. It is priced in US dollars: $29 a month for Creator and $99 a month for Creator Pro, with a 14-day free trial. It does not handle brand deals, invoices or a media kit. Read our full Brandzy vs Stan Store comparison.

3. Linktree: best free list of links

Linktree is the best-known link-in-bio page, with a free plan and paid upgrades in US dollars. It can sell digital products, but lower plans charge a seller fee on sales. Good if you only need a tidy list of links.

4. Beacons: free all-in-one with a media kit

Beacons has a free plan, a store and a media kit feature, with paid plans in US dollars. Lower plans charge a seller fee on product sales. It does not include a brand deal pipeline or invoicing.

How to choose

  1. Count where your income comes from. If brand deals are a big share, you need deal tracking and invoices, not just a store.
  2. Work out the real cost in rupees. Include seller fees and the exchange rate on USD plans.
  3. Check how you get paid. Make sure payments reach your Indian bank account without extra steps.
