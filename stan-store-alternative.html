Store, checkout, courses, email list, broadcasts and automations are all in Brandzy. On top of that you get a second half that Stan Store does not list at all: the tools to run your brand deals. In every row of the table where Stan says "Not listed", Brandzy says yes.

Why Indian creators switch to Brandzy

1. Brand deals are most of the money

For many Indian YouTubers and Instagram creators, sponsorships earn more than product sales. Stan Store is built around selling to fans. Brandzy tracks every sponsorship from the first message to the final payment: inquiry, negotiation, contract, delivery and paid.

2. Brands reach you through your media kit

Your Brandzy link shows your audience numbers, rate card and the brands you have worked with. When a brand fills in the inquiry form, Brandzy creates the deal for you and notifies you, even when the app is closed.

3. Invoices and follow-ups without spreadsheets

Create an invoice from a deal, send it by email or WhatsApp, see what is due or overdue, and send payment reminders. Mark it paid and the deal moves to Paid by itself.

4. Pay in rupees

Brandzy costs ₹999 a month and you pay by UPI, card or net banking. Tools priced in US dollars cost more when the rupee falls, and some Indian cards add a foreign transaction charge.

5. Sell anything from the same link

The Brandzy store has 17 categories: digital downloads, e-books, online courses, templates, presets, music, art, bundles, 1:1 coaching calls, consultations, workshops, memberships, services, physical merch, affiliate links and "other". Fans pay you by UPI straight to your own UPI ID, so there is no payout wait.

6. Email that sells for you

Everyone who buys from you or joins your list lands in your email list. Send broadcasts, or set up automations once: a welcome series for new subscribers and follow-ups after someone buys, sent days apart on their own.

Outside India? Brandzy still costs less

Creators outside India pay US$12 a month for Growth or US$24 for Pro, by card. That is less than Stan Store's $29 Creator plan, with the brand-deal tools on top. See Linktree vs Stan Store vs Brandzy for the full cost table.

Where Stan Store is still ahead (for now)

  • Automatic card confirmation. Stan confirms card payments automatically. On Brandzy, buyers outside India pay by card through your own Razorpay, Stripe or PayPal payment link, and you confirm the order in one tap once you see the payment.

If neither of those is essential for you right now, Brandzy gives you more for less.

Moving from Stan Store to Brandzy

  1. Start the 7-day free trial and pick your username. That becomes your Brandzy link.
