Frequently asked questions

Does Brandzy take a commission on sales?

No. Brandzy charges a flat monthly price and takes 0% of your sales. Buyers pay you directly.

How does UPI checkout work?

The buyer pays to your UPI ID from any UPI app. You confirm the payment in Brandzy and the buyer's access unlocks. Free products unlock instantly.

Can I sell to buyers outside India?

Yes. Add your own Razorpay, Stripe or PayPal payment link for card payments; you confirm the payment, then access unlocks.
