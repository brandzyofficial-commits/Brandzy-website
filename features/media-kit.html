Frequently asked questions

What is a live media kit?

A web page instead of a PDF: your numbers and rates stay current, and brands can send an inquiry from the page.

Are my stats verified?

On Pro, Brandzy reads your numbers from the official Instagram and YouTube APIs every day and marks them Verified.

Can I still send a PDF?

Yes. Make a one-page PDF with the free media kit maker, and use the live link for everything else.
