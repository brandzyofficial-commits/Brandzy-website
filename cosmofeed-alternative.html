Which should you choose?

Cosmofeed: Choose Cosmofeed if you want card and UPI payments confirmed automatically by a gateway, or sell only occasionally, so a per-sale fee is cheaper than a monthly price.

Brandzy: Choose Brandzy if you sell every month (the flat price wins above roughly ₹8,500), want money straight to your UPI ID, or also run brand deals and Instagram Auto DM.
