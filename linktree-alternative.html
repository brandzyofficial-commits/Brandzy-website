What Linktree's seller fee really costs

Say you sell ₹50,000 of e-books, presets or courses a month. On Linktree's free plan, a 12% seller fee takes about ₹6,000 of it. On Starter or Pro, 9% takes about ₹4,500 on top of the subscription. On Brandzy you pay ₹999 a month and keep every rupee of your sales, at any volume. See the full Linktree vs Stan Store vs Brandzy cost table.

Why creators in India outgrow Linktree

1. Your link in bio should earn, not just point

A list of links sends followers somewhere else to buy. Brandzy puts the store on the page itself: followers pick a product, pay by UPI from any UPI app, and the money lands in your bank account. You confirm the payment and they get their download, course or booking.

2. Brand deals need more than a link

Most creator income in India still comes from sponsorships. Brandzy's public media kit shows your audience numbers, rate card and the brands you have worked with, with a form brands use to pitch you. Each pitch becomes a deal in your pipeline with a notification, so nothing gets lost in DMs. From there you track deliverables, send content for approval, and send the invoice by email or WhatsApp.

3. Paying in rupees, without seller fees

Brandzy is priced in rupees and you pay by UPI, card or net banking, so there is no exchange-rate surprise or foreign transaction charge. There is no commission on what you sell or on your brand deals.

Frequently asked questions

Does Brandzy take a cut of my sales?

No. It is a flat monthly subscription. Buyers pay you directly by UPI. Linktree's seller fee is 12% on its free plan and 9% on Starter and Pro.

Is Brandzy better than Linktree?

For creators who sell or work with brands, yes: a store with UPI checkout, courses, bookings, memberships and Auto DM with 0% commission, plus a media kit, deal pipeline and invoices Linktree does not have, from ₹999 a month (US$12 outside India). If you only need a free list of links, Linktree is enough.

Is there a free plan?

There is a 7-day free trial with every feature. After that, Growth is ₹999 a month and Pro is ₹1,999 a month. See pricing.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The Brandzy Android app is on Google Play (search “Brandzy” or open it on Google Play), with the same account, dashboard and plan as Brandzy on the web. On iPhone, Brandzy for iPhone installs from Safari in about 10 seconds with no App Store download; it opens full screen from its own icon with the same account and plan. A native App Store version is on the way.
