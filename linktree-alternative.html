Why creators in India outgrow Linktree

1. Your link in bio should earn, not just point

A list of links sends followers somewhere else to buy. Brandzy puts the store on the page itself: followers pick a product, pay by UPI from any UPI app, and the money lands in your bank account. You confirm the payment and they get their download, course or booking.

2. Brand deals need more than a link

Most creator income in India still comes from sponsorships. Brandzy's public media kit shows your audience numbers, rate card and the brands you have worked with, with a form brands use to pitch you. Each pitch becomes a deal in your pipeline with a notification, so nothing gets lost in DMs. From there you track deliverables, send content for approval, and send the invoice by email or WhatsApp.

3. Paying in rupees, without seller fees

Brandzy is priced in rupees and you pay by UPI, card or net banking, so there is no exchange-rate surprise or foreign transaction charge. There is no commission on what you sell or on your brand deals.
