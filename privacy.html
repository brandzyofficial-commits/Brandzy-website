1. What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains what information Brandzy ("we", "us", "our") collects when you use the Brandzy app and website, and how it is used, stored, and protected.

2. Data stored in the app

Brandzy is built to keep your deal, client, invoice, and media kit data on your own device, in a local database. We do not require an account to use the core features, and we do not transmit your deal or client data to our servers as part of normal app use. [If a future version adds optional cloud sync/backup, this section will be updated to describe what is uploaded, where it's stored, and how to opt out.]

3. Information you provide to us directly

If you contact us (e.g. by email at info@brandzy.app) or sign up for early access, we collect the information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address and the contents of your message.

4. Website data

This website (brandzy.app) does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. Standard web server logs (such as IP address and browser type) may be collected by our hosting provider for security and operational purposes.

5. How we use information

  • To operate and improve the Service;
  • To respond to support requests sent to us directly;
  • To send early-access or product updates, only if you opted in.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share data you enter into the app with third parties, because that data is stored locally on your device and is not accessible to us. Information you send us directly (e.g. support emails) is not shared except as required by law.

7. Data security

Since deal, client, and invoice data lives on your device, its security depends in part on your device's own security (passcode, encryption, biometric lock). Brandzy supports an in-app biometric/passcode lock as an additional layer of protection.

8. Your choices

You can delete any deal, client, invoice, or media kit entry directly within the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes its local data from your device, subject to your device's own backup settings.

9. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to info@brandzy.app.
