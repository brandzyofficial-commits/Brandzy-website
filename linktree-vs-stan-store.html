Linktree vs Stan Store: the real difference

Linktree started as a tidy list of links, and that is still what it does best. It can sell digital products, but the lower plans take a share of every sale, so the free plan gets expensive as soon as you start earning.

Stan Store is built to sell: a one-page store with checkout, courses, bookings, email marketing and Instagram AutoDM, with no cut of your sales. The trade-off is price. At $29 or $99 a month, billed in US dollars, it is a big fixed cost for a smaller creator.

Both stop at the fan side of the business. Neither helps with sponsorships, which for most creators are a large share of income.

Store, checkout, courses, bookings, memberships, an email list and Instagram Auto DM, from US$12 a month (₹999 in India), with no commission at any sales volume.

2. The brand side neither of them has

Your Brandzy link is also a live media kit: audience numbers, rate card and the brands you have worked with. When a brand fills in the inquiry form, Brandzy creates the deal and notifies you. 3. Built for India, and works everywhere

Indian creators pay in rupees by UPI, card or net banking, and buyers pay them by UPI straight to their own UPI ID. Creators and buyers outside India pay by card. There is no foreign transaction charge and no payout wait.

When to choose the others
