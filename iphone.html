Questions

Is this the real Brandzy?

Yes. It is the full Brandzy app: brand deals, invoices, CRM, media kit and your store, with the same account and plan as the Android app and brandzy.app on a computer.

Why isn't it on the App Store yet?

We launched on the web and Android first. A native iPhone app is in the works. Until then, this is the fastest way to use Brandzy on an iPhone, and your account carries over when the App Store version arrives.

I opened this link from Instagram and there's no Add to Home Screen

Apps like Instagram open links in their own browser, which can't add apps to your home screen. Tap ••• and choose Open in external browser (or copy the link and paste it into Safari), then follow the steps above.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Any iPhone on iOS 18.2 or later. To check, open Settings › General › About; to update, open Settings › General › Software Update.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. The steps are the same; the Share button is at the top right of Safari on iPad.
