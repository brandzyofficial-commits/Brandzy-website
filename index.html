Frequently Asked Questions

Brandzy is a web app for independent creators and influencers that keeps brand deals, invoices, a client CRM, deliverables, a live media kit and a creator storefront in one place, behind one shareable link. It is built for creators in India and works in any browser. It costs a flat monthly fee (₹999 or ₹1,999, or $12 or $24 outside India) and never takes a commission on your earnings.
Brandzy is for YouTubers, Instagram creators and other influencers who work with brands and want to stop tracking sponsorships in spreadsheets and chat threads. It suits solo creators and small creator teams, from first brand deal to regular campaigns.
Brandzy is a flat monthly subscription — never a commission. Payments from your brand deals and store sales go directly from the brand or customer to you; Brandzy never sits in the middle of that money, so there’s no cut taken and no percentage fee.
Yes. Every creator gets one 7-day free trial with full access. Cancel before it ends and you pay nothing. When the trial ends, pick Growth (₹999/month, or $12 outside India) or Pro (₹1,999/month, or $24 outside India) to keep using your dashboard. Your data stays saved either way.
Creators in India pay in INR through Cashfree Payments (UPI, cards, and net banking), as prepaid packs of 1, 3, 6 or 12 months (12 months costs the same as 10). Nothing renews automatically. Creators outside India are billed in USD through our international payment partner, which acts as the merchant of record and handles local sales tax/VAT. There is nothing to cancel: your plan simply ends unless you pay again.
Add a product, pick one of 17 categories, set a price and switch it live. You can sell digital downloads, e-books and guides, online courses, templates, presets and filters, music or audio, art and wallpapers, bundles, 1:1 coaching calls, consultations, workshops and webinars, memberships and communities, services (shoutouts, reviews, edits), physical products and merch, affiliate or recommended links, a tip jar, or anything else. Each category only asks for what it needs, such as a download link or booking slots. Products appear on your public Brandzy page next to your media kit, so fans and brands use the same link.
Yes. Brandzy does what Stan Store does for selling to fans, and then runs the brand side of your business too. On the store side you get a link-in-bio storefront with 17 product categories, built-in checkout where fans pay you by UPI straight to your own UPI ID (no commission, no payout wait), a course builder, an email list with broadcasts, and email automations such as a welcome series and after-purchase follow-ups. On the brand side, which Stan Store does not offer, you get a sponsorship pipeline, invoices with payment reminders, a client CRM, deliverable deadlines and a live media kit whose inquiry form turns brand messages into deals automatically. Brandzy costs ₹999 or ₹1,999 a month in rupees, while Stan Store costs $29 or $99 a month in US dollars. Instagram Auto DM and card checkout are coming soon; until then Stan Store is the pick if those two are essential for you.

From there you negotiate, track deliverables, send your content to the brand for approval and raise an invoice from the same deal.
