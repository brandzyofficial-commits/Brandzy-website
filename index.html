Deal pipeline

Move every brand deal through Outreach → Negotiating → Contracted → Delivered → Paid, and always know what's next.

🧾

Invoicing

👥

Client CRM

✅

Deliverables tracking

Smart reminders

How it works

From first DM to paid invoice

1

Log the deal

A brand slides into your DMs — add it to Brandzy in seconds with rate, deliverables, and deadline.

2

Track it through the pipeline

Watch the deal move from negotiating to contracted, with every deliverable checked off as you go.

3

Send the invoice

Generate an invoice directly from the deal and share it with the brand in one tap.

4

Get paid, stay organized

Mark it paid, and your dashboard, CRM, and media kit numbers update automatically.
