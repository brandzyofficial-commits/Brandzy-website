Reel reward terms

  1. The Reel must be your own original content, posted from your own public Instagram account with at least 50,000 followers, and stay up for at least 30 days.
  2. Only views in the first 30 days after posting count. We check them from the Reel's Instagram insights and may ask for a screenshot or a screen recording.
  3. Use your own link, brandzy.app/?ref=yourhandle (your Instagram handle without the @), so we can see who joins Brandzy from your Reel.
  4. A free month is a benefit, so you must disclose it: use Instagram's paid partnership label or add #ad or #collab, as the ASCI influencer guidelines ask.
  5. Say only what's true about Brandzy, for example: 0% commission on your sales, UPI checkout, a 7-day free trial, plans from ₹999 a month.
  6. No bought views, bots, giveaways for views or engagement groups. Reels that use them don't qualify.
  7. One reward per creator per calendar month, and at most three rewards per creator in total.
  8. Up to 50 rewards are given each calendar month across all campaigns, first come, first served. We may pause or end a campaign at any time; rewards already earned are honoured.
  9. The reward is a month of a Brandzy plan added to the account with your email. It has no cash value and can't be transferred.
  10. With your permission, we may reshare your Reel on Brandzy's own accounts, with credit to you.
  11. Rewards are run by Tharun Tech Solutions, Hanamkonda, Warangal. Questions: info@brandzy.app.

Frequently asked questions

What do I get for posting a Reel about Brandzy?

A free month of Brandzy Growth (worth ₹999) or Pro (worth ₹1,999), depending on the campaign, once your Reel passes the campaign's view count within 30 days of posting. The reward is a plan month, not cash.

Who can join?

Creators with at least 50,000 Instagram followers, posting from their own public account.

How many views does my Reel need?

10,000 views for a review Reel, 25,000 for a how-to Reel and 10,000 for a Reel in an Indian language other than English, all within 30 days of posting.

Do I need to disclose that the Reel is rewarded?

Yes. A free month is a benefit, so label the Reel as a paid partnership or add #ad or #collab, as India's ASCI influencer guidelines ask.

How do you check my views?

Send the Reel link and the view count with this form. We may ask for a screenshot or a screen recording of the Reel's insights before we add the free month.
