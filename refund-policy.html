Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This is a template draft. Once paid plans launch, replace the bracketed placeholders with your actual refund window, pricing, and store-specific terms, and have it reviewed by a qualified professional before publishing.

1. Current status: early access

Brandzy is currently in early access and free to use. No payments are currently collected for the Brandzy app itself, so no refunds are applicable at this time. This policy will take effect once paid plans are introduced.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers only fees you pay to Brandzy for the app itself (e.g. a future subscription or pro plan). It does not cover payments you receive from brands for your own work — Brandzy does not process, hold, or refund money exchanged between you and a brand; those payments and any related disputes are between you and the brand directly.

3. Subscriptions purchased through an app store

If you subscribe to a paid Brandzy plan through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, that purchase is governed by the respective store's refund and billing policies, and refund requests must be submitted through that store (Apple or Google), not directly to Brandzy. We are not able to override app store refund decisions.

4. Cancellation

You may cancel a paid subscription at any time through your app store account settings (Apple ID / Google Play subscriptions). Cancelling stops future billing; it does not retroactively refund the current billing period unless required by the app store's policy or applicable law.

5. Effect of cancellation on your data

Cancelling a paid plan does not delete your locally stored deal, client, or invoice data. Any features exclusive to the paid plan will no longer be accessible once the current billing period ends.

6. Direct refund requests

For purchases not made through an app store (e.g. a future direct web purchase), refund requests can be sent to info@brandzy.app within [X] days of the charge, and will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as paid features are introduced. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact

Billing questions can be sent to info@brandzy.app.
