Which should you choose?

Instamojo: Choose Instamojo if you need a full payment gateway with automatic confirmation, GST invoices from the gateway, or you sell physical products at volume.

Brandzy: Choose Brandzy if you're a creator selling digital products, courses or calls and want 0% commission, plus Auto DM, a media kit and brand deals in the same place.

Frequently asked questions

What does Instamojo charge on digital products?

Instamojo's pricing page lists 10% + ₹3 per digital product sale on its free store plan, lower on paid plans, with 18% GST on fees (checked 15 November 2026). Its plan figures vary across the page, so confirm on Instamojo before deciding.

Is Brandzy a payment gateway?

No. Buyers pay you directly by UPI (or your own Razorpay, Stripe or PayPal link), and you confirm the payment in Brandzy. That's why Brandzy takes no commission.
