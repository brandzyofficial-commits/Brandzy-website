Frequently asked questions

How many Instagram Auto DM rules can I have on each plan?

Growth includes 1 active Auto DM rule and Pro includes unlimited rules. The 7-day free trial includes every Pro feature, so you can try several rules first.

How does Brandzy's Instagram Auto DM work?

You pick a keyword and a message. When someone comments that keyword on your post or Reel, or sends it to you in a DM, Brandzy sends them your message with your link, through the official Instagram API.

How do I connect my Instagram account to Brandzy?

Open Store → Auto DM → Connect Instagram and log in on Instagram's own page. You need an Instagram professional (Business or Creator) account; switching is free in Instagram's settings.

Is Brandzy safe for my Instagram account?

Yes. Brandzy uses the official Instagram API, approved by Meta, and you connect through Instagram's own login. It doesn't use bots, scraping or your password.

How does UPI checkout work in a Brandzy store?

The buyer pays to your UPI ID from any UPI app, then you confirm the payment in Brandzy (Store → Orders → Mark as paid) and their access unlocks. Free products unlock instantly. The money goes straight to you.

Can I sell to buyers outside India?

Yes. Add your own Razorpay, Stripe or PayPal payment link for card payments. The buyer pays you there, you confirm the payment in Brandzy, and their access unlocks.

Does Brandzy take a commission on my sales?

No. Brandzy charges a flat monthly price and takes 0% of your sales and brand payments. Buyers and brands pay you directly.

How do paid 1:1 call bookings work with Google Calendar?

Connect Google Calendar in Brandzy and create a call product. Buyers pick a free slot, and each paid booking becomes a Google Calendar event with a Google Meet link. Your busy times are hidden from buyers.

What's the difference between Brandzy Growth and Pro?

Growth (₹999/month) has the store with UPI checkout, courses, media kit, brand deals, CRM, invoices, email list and 1 Auto DM rule. Pro (₹1,999/month) adds email broadcasts and automations, unlimited Auto DM rules, verified Instagram and YouTube stats, and priority support.

How does the Brandzy free trial work?

New accounts get one 7-day free trial with every Pro feature. Nothing is charged until the trial ends, and if you turn auto-renew off during the trial you're never charged. After the trial, an active plan is needed to keep using your dashboard.

How do I pay for Brandzy?

In India, buy a pack of 1, 3, 6 or 12 months once through Cashfree (UPI, cards, net banking), or pay on auto-renew through Razorpay (UPI Autopay or card). 12 months costs the same as 10. Outside India, pay in US dollars by card through Razorpay.

How do I turn off auto-renew?

Open Settings and tap Turn off auto-renew. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again. You can also email info@brandzy.app.

Can I get a refund on Brandzy?

Yes, within 14 days of your first subscription payment or of any renewal charge, if it's your first refund request for that plan. Refunds go back to your original payment method, usually in 5–7 business days.

What happens when a brand sends an inquiry from my media kit?

The inquiry becomes a new deal in your brand deal pipeline automatically, and you get a notification, even while the app is closed. From there you track it through negotiation, contract, delivery and payment.

What are verified stats on the media kit?

On Pro, Brandzy reads your Instagram and YouTube numbers from the official APIs every day and shows them on your media kit with a Verified badge, so brands can trust them.

Is there a Brandzy app for Android and iPhone?

Yes. The Android app is on Google Play. On iPhone, add Brandzy to your home screen from Safari in about 10 seconds, no App Store download needed; a native iPhone app is on the way. It's the same account and plan everywhere, and Brandzy also works in any browser.

How do I delete my Brandzy account?

Open Settings and tap Delete account. This deletes your account and its data. If you're on auto-renew, turn it off first so you aren't charged again.
