How do I turn off auto-renew?

Plans and billing · Updated 15 November 2026

Short answer

Open Settings and tap Turn off auto-renew. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again. You can also email info@brandzy.app.

Turning off auto-renew stops future charges. It doesn't delete your account or your store.
