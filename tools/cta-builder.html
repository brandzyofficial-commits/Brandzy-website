How to write a call to action that works

  • Ask for one thing. "Comment GOA" works better than "like, comment, share and follow".
  • Say what they get and, if it's paid, the price.
  • Make it easy: a keyword comment beats "link in bio", because the link comes to them.
  • Repeat it in the caption, on screen at the end of the Reel and in your bio.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good call to action for Instagram?

One clear action with a reason: “Comment GOA and I'll DM you the itinerary” or “Get the guide from the link in my bio”. Ask for one thing per post.

Do comment-keyword CTAs really work?

Yes, they usually get more comments than “link in bio”, and comments help reach. Pair them with an Auto DM tool so the link is sent automatically.
