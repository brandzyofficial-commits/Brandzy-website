How to plan your Instagram grid

  • Instagram's profile grid shows 4:5 tiles (taller than square), so check that faces and text aren't cut off at the top and bottom.
  • Alternate close-ups with wide shots, and text posts with photos, so no two neighbours look the same.
  • Keep a colour thread: similar editing or a repeated accent colour makes a profile look intentional.
  • Pin your best three posts (a store, a freebie, a top Reel) to the top row.

Frequently asked questions

What size are Instagram grid tiles now?

Instagram's profile grid shows posts as 4:5 tiles (for example 1080 × 1350 pixels). Square 1:1 posts are cropped at the sides in the grid.

Are my photos uploaded when I use the grid planner?

No. Photos are read by your browser and never leave your device. Reloading the page clears them.
