What this invoice includes

  • Your name, address, PAN and GSTIN (if you have one), and the brand's details.
  • Each deliverable as its own line, so the brand's finance team can match it to the agreement.
  • GST as separate lines: 18% IGST if the brand is in another state, or 9% CGST + 9% SGST if it's in your state. If you aren't registered, no GST is added and it's titled "Invoice", not "Tax Invoice".
  • The total in words (Indian format, in lakh and crore), due date and your UPI or bank details.

For the full checklist, GST thresholds and why brands pay you less than the invoice (TDS), read how to invoice brands as an influencer in India. This tool gives general formatting help, not tax advice; check your situation with a chartered accountant.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make an influencer invoice without GST registration?

Yes. Choose "Not GST-registered" and the invoice will not add GST and will be titled "Invoice". Do not charge GST if you are not registered.

Which GST applies to a brand deal, IGST or CGST and SGST?

If you are GST-registered and the brand is in a different state, charge 18% IGST. If the brand is in the same state as you, charge 9% CGST plus 9% SGST. Check the details with your chartered accountant.

How do I save the invoice as a PDF?

Press Download PDF and choose "Save as PDF" as the printer. On Android, choose "Save as PDF" in the print screen; on iPhone, use the share button in the print preview.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. The invoice is built in your browser. Your details are remembered only on this device so you don't have to retype them next time.
