What makes a good Reel hook

  • Say it in the first 1 to 2 seconds, out loud and as on-screen text. Many people watch with the sound off.
  • Name who it's for ("If you're a college student…"), so the right people stop.
  • Open a loop: a question, mistake or result the viewer needs the rest of the Reel to resolve.
  • Use specifics: numbers, prices and places beat vague promises.
  • Deliver on it. A hook that over-promises loses people, and that hurts reach more than a weak hook.

Frequently asked questions

What is a hook in an Instagram Reel?

The first 1 to 3 seconds: the line you say and show on screen that makes someone stop scrolling and keep watching.

Is this hook generator AI?

No. It fills in proven hook templates with your topic and audience, so it's instant, free and private. Edit the lines so they sound like you.

How long should a Reel hook be?

Short enough to say in about 2 seconds, usually 6 to 12 words, and shown as on-screen text too.
