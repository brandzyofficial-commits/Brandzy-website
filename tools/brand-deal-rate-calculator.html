How this calculator works

Brands increasingly pay for views, not followers, so the price starts from your average views and a rate per 1,000 views (often called CPM). It then adjusts for:

  • Niche: finance, tech and business audiences are worth more to advertisers than broad entertainment audiences.
  • Engagement: a higher engagement rate (likes, comments, saves and shares compared with followers) adds up to 20%.
  • Usage rights: if the brand runs your content as a paid ad, they get far more value from it, so charge more.
  • Exclusivity and rush: turning down competitors or dropping your schedule costs you, so it's priced in.

The starting rates per 1,000 views are Brandzy's assumptions, shown openly so you can change them. Rates vary a lot by brand, season and agency, so treat the result as a starting point for your quote.

Tips for quoting brands

  1. Quote first, and quote high. Most brands negotiate down, so start near the top of your range.
  2. Price each deliverable. "1 reel + 3 stories" is easier to negotiate than one lump sum.
  3. Put usage rights in writing: how long the brand may use your content, where, and whether as ads.
  4. Get paid on time: agree payment terms before you post, then send a proper invoice. Use the free invoice generator.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I charge for an Instagram reel in India?

Start from your average reel views, not your followers. Multiply your average views by a rate per 1,000 views, then adjust for your niche, engagement and how the brand will use the content. This calculator does that math and shows each step, so you can see where the number comes from.

Should I charge by followers or by views?

By views where you can. Followers show your reach on paper, but views show how many people actually see a sponsored post, which is what brands pay for. If your views are much lower than your followers, expect brands to price on views.

How much extra should I charge if the brand runs my reel as an ad?

Many creators add 30% to 100% for paid ad usage, depending on how long and where the brand will run it. This calculator adds 50% by default. Agree the duration in writing, for example 3 months on Meta ads.

Is this calculator's rate what brands will pay?

It is a starting point for your quote, not a guarantee. Rates depend on the brand, the season, the agency and your past results. Change the starting rate per 1,000 views to match deals you have already done.
