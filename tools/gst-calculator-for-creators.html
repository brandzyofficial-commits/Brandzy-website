How GST works on brand deals

Formulas

Adding GST: total = amount × (1 + rate). ₹25,000 + 18% = ₹29,500.

Removing GST: amount before GST = total ÷ (1 + rate). ₹29,500 ÷ 1.18 = ₹25,000.

If the brand is in the same state as you, the GST is split equally into CGST and SGST (9% + 9% at 18%). If it's in another state, it's charged as IGST (18%). You need a GST registration to charge GST; it becomes compulsory once your yearly turnover from services crosses ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in some states), and brands often prefer registered creators. Frequently asked questions

What is the GST rate for influencers and content creators in India?

Advertising, promotion and content creation services usually carry 18% GST: 9% CGST plus 9% SGST for a brand in your state, or 18% IGST for a brand in another state.

When does a creator need GST registration?

Once your yearly turnover from services crosses ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in some special category states). You can register earlier if brands ask for a GST invoice.

How do I remove GST from a total amount?

Divide the total by 1 plus the rate. For 18% GST, ₹29,500 ÷ 1.18 = ₹25,000 before GST, and ₹4,500 is the GST.
