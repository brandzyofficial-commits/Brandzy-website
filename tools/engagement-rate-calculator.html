How engagement rate is calculated

Formula

Engagement rate by followers = (likes + comments + saves + shares) ÷ number of posts ÷ followers × 100

Engagement rate by views = total interactions ÷ total views × 100

Using 5 to 10 recent posts gives a fairer number than one viral post. Engagement by followers is what most brands ask for. Engagement by views shows how well each post lands with the people who actually saw it, which matters more for reels that reach non-followers.

What's a good engagement rate?

There's no official benchmark, and it changes with account size: accounts with a few thousand followers often see 4–8%, while very large accounts often see 1–2%. As a rough guide, under 1% is low, 1–3% is average, 3–6% is good and above 6% is high.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my Instagram engagement rate?

Add up the likes, comments, saves and shares on your last 5 to 10 posts, divide by the number of posts to get the average, then divide by your followers and multiply by 100.

What is a good engagement rate on Instagram in India?

As a rough guide, 1 to 3 percent is average, 3 to 6 percent is good and above 6 percent is high. Smaller accounts usually have higher rates than large ones.

Should I use engagement by followers or by views?

Brands most often ask for engagement by followers. Engagement by views is useful for reels and YouTube, where many viewers are not followers. This calculator shows both.
