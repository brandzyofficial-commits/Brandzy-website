    How many hashtags should you use?

    Instagram allows up to 30 hashtags on a post and recommends 3 to 5. Hashtags now help Instagram understand what your post is about more than they bring reach on their own, so pick a few that describe the topic exactly (#goatrip, #budgettravelindia) rather than broad ones like #reels or #viral. Hashtags aren't case-sensitive, so #GoaTrip and #goatrip are the same tag.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many hashtags can you use on Instagram?

    Up to 30 on a post. Instagram recommends 3 to 5 relevant hashtags.

    Are Instagram hashtags case-sensitive?

    No. #GoaTrip and #goatrip are the same hashtag, so this tool removes duplicates that differ only in capital letters.

    Do broad hashtags like #viral or #explorepage help?

    Not really. They're too broad to tell Instagram what your post is about. Use specific hashtags that describe your topic.
