    What makes a good Instagram bio

    1. Say who it's for in the first line. "Budget travel tips from India" beats "Wanderer ✨ Dreamer".
    2. Give a reason to follow: what people get and how often, like "New recipe every Sunday".
    3. End with one call to action that points to your link, like "👇 Free itinerary" or "👇 Book a 1:1 call".
    4. Make brand contact easy: add an email or "Collabs: DM 'COLLAB'".
    5. Use one link that holds everything: your store, free guide, media kit and socials on one page.

    Instagram allows 150 characters in the bio and 30 in the name field. The name field is searchable, so adding a keyword ("Riya | Skincare") helps the right people find you.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many characters can an Instagram bio have?

    150 characters for the bio and 30 characters for the name field. Emojis count as characters, and some count as two.

    Are links in the Instagram bio text clickable?

    No. Only the link field (and other accounts tagged with @) are clickable. Put your link in the link field and point to it with a call to action like "👇 Free guide".

    What should I put in my Instagram bio as a creator?

    Who your content is for, what people get by following, one call to action, a way for brands to contact you, and one link that holds your store, media kit and offers.
