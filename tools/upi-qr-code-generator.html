Frequently asked questions

Is this UPI QR code generator free?

Yes. It is free, needs no sign-up, and the QR code works with any UPI app. Payments go directly to your UPI ID.

Can I set a fixed amount in a UPI QR code?

Yes. Enter the amount and the QR code asks the payer to pay exactly that. Leave it empty if you want the payer to type the amount.

Is it safe to share my UPI ID and QR code?

Sharing your UPI ID or a QR code to receive money is normal and safe. Never share your UPI PIN, and remember that you never need to enter your PIN to receive money.

Why doesn't the UPI link open on my computer?

UPI pay links open UPI apps, which are on phones. On a computer, share or print the QR code image instead.
