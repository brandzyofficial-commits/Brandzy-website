Best video size for Reels, Shorts and TikTok

Quick answer

1080 × 1920 pixels, 9:16 vertical, MP4 (H.264) works full screen on Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok.

  • Keep text and faces inside the safe zone. The username, caption and buttons cover the bottom and right of the screen.
  • Length: Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts allow up to 3 minutes; TikTok allows longer. Shorter videos usually hold viewers better.
  • Cover image: the profile grid shows a 4:5 crop of your Reel, so keep the cover's key part in the middle.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best video size for Instagram Reels?

1080 × 1920 pixels in a 9:16 vertical ratio, exported as MP4 with H.264. The same file works for YouTube Shorts and TikTok.

What is the Reels safe zone?

The middle part of the screen that isn't covered by the username, caption and buttons. Keep text and faces away from the bottom fifth and the right edge.

Is my video uploaded when I check it?

No. Your browser reads the file on your device to get its size and length. Nothing is uploaded.
