What brands look for in a media kit

  • Real, recent numbers: average views and engagement from your last 10 posts, not your best ever.
  • Who your audience is: top cities, age and gender, from your Instagram or YouTube insights.
  • Clear rates per deliverable, so brands can budget quickly.
  • Proof: brands you've worked with, and an easy way to contact you.

A PDF is a good start, but it goes out of date the week you send it. For the full guide, read how to make an influencer media kit.

Frequently asked questions

What should an influencer media kit include?

Your photo and a short bio, your handles, recent average views and engagement rate, audience details (top cities, age, gender), your rates per deliverable, brands you have worked with and a contact email.

Is this media kit maker free?

Yes. Fill in your details, add a photo and save it as a PDF. No sign-up, and your photo never leaves your device.

How often should I update my media kit?

Every month, or before you pitch a brand, so your numbers are current. Brandzy's live media kit updates your Instagram and YouTube numbers automatically every day.
