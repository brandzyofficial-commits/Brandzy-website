A simple formula for an Instagram bio

  1. Line 1: what you post and who it's for.
  2. Line 2: proof or how often you post.
  3. Line 3: how brands can reach you.
  4. Line 4: one call to action pointing at your link.

Keep it within 150 characters. Bios over the limit show a red border above. Need more call-to-action ideas? Try the call-to-action builder.
