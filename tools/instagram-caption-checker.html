    Instagram caption limits

    • 2,200 characters per caption. Emojis count, and some count as two.
    • About 125 characters show in the feed before "more", so the hook goes first.
    • 30 hashtags at most; Instagram suggests 3 to 5 relevant ones. Check them with the hashtag counter.
    • 20 @mentions at most.
    • Links aren't clickable in captions: point to the link in your bio, or send it automatically by DM when people comment.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the Instagram caption character limit?

    2,200 characters. Only about the first 125 show in the feed before “more”, so put your hook first.

    How many hashtags can I use on Instagram?

    Up to 30 per post, but Instagram recommends 3 to 5 relevant hashtags.

    Are links clickable in Instagram captions?

    No. Point people to the link in your bio, or use an Auto DM tool to send the link when they comment a keyword.
